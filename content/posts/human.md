**What is it to be a human?**
To feel cold under our feet and hungry from time to time. 
To repeat someone's name over and over again.
To measure the length, weigth and depth of every thing and been.
<!--more-->
To taka a deep breath and feel the here and now.
To break the chains we put on ourselves.
To say 'I love you' in every language , every color, every rhythm and every scent.

To use our thumbs and learn from our mistakes. 
To be amazed by just seeing a landscape.
To wonder what's beyond stars.

To sketch the shape of our soul.
To find out the secrets of the light. 
To recognice human's fragility and make it  our greatest strength.
To say 'I forgive you' every now and then.
