How good that **flower** smelled, 
Velvety buttery touch. 
Surrenders between my fingers, 
A scent like fruit punch. 
<!--more-->
A desperate tongue-
The flower drips an elixir. 
Every sip and lick 
Feels like getting young.

Is this how ambrosia tastes? 
I just want to fill my mouth, 
But I'll take my time,
I won't make haste.

I hope everyone could taste this,
The famine eradicated,
Every war brouht to an end- 
That's my prayer and my wish.
